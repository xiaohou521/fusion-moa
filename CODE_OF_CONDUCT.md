# Community code of conduct

Be respectful, specific, and evidence-driven. Welcome contributors regardless
of background or affiliation. Critique ideas and results, not people. Do not
harass, threaten, discriminate, expose private information, or pressure anyone
to disclose proprietary data or credentials.

Maintainers may edit or remove abusive content and restrict participation when
needed to protect the community. Report conduct concerns privately to the
repository maintainers; they should acknowledge the report, avoid conflicts of
interest, and communicate the resolution.
